## 🚀 Overview

Traditional cultural tourism prioritizes visitors, leaving local custodians without control over their stories, access, and financial value[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span). When culture travels without its custodians, meaning and value travel away[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

**Custodian** is a custodian-first digital platform designed for discovering, experiencing, and preserving living culture on its own terms[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). Grounded in the principle that **"AI translates culture; it does not invent it,"**[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span) the platform ensures that consent, native language preservation, context, and fair value return are embedded into every cultural interaction[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

---

## 💡 Key Features

* **Custodian-Controlled Consent:** Custodians define what can be seen, shared, or booked[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span). Consent acts as a first-class data layer where scope, audience, reuse rights, and revenue rules travel alongside every story[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
* **Native-Language Storytelling:** Custodians record field notes in their native regional dialects, preserving their authentic voice rather than a diluted summary[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
* **AI-Assisted Multilingual Access:** RAG-grounded translation allows travellers to listen and read in their preferred language without eroding cultural authority[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
* **Place-Led Map Discovery:** Interactive spatial discovery introduces relationships and context rather than extracting content[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
* **Direct Participation & Monetization:** Experience bookings, craft commissions, and support mechanisms return financial value directly to knowledge holders[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
